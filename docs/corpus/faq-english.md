# FAQ (English)

**How much does the current account cost?**
The Girokonto Komfort costs 4.90 € per month, including a debit card and
free SEPA transfers in online banking.

**What does a replacement debit card cost?**
A replacement debit card costs 10.00 € and arrives within 5 business days.

**How do I block a lost card?**
Call the central blocking hotline 116 116 (from abroad: +49 116 116) or
use online banking under "Cards > Block card".

**Why was my loan application declined?**
Common reasons are negative SCHUFA entries, insufficient disposable income,
or employment still within the probation period.
